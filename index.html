Then & Now

Bird records from around your area in 1870–1939 (mostly museum specimens and old collections), compared with 2015 to today. Shows birds common back then that are rare now, and newcomers your great-grandparents never saw.

Location

eBird key (free)

Needed for "Who to expect", Rarity run and Feeder planner. The forecast, Sounds, Yard ledger and Then & Now work without it.
To get one: sign in (or make a free account) at ebird.org, then open ebird.org/api/keygen ↗. Agree to the terms, and copy the short key it shows into the box below.

Data: eBird (Cornell Lab), Open-Meteo weather, GBIF historical records, and Wikimedia Commons recordings (many from xeno-canto). The migration forecast is a weather-based estimate: helpful, not gospel. Everything you log stays on this phone. Use Backup to keep a copy.
